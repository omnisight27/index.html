What is the Fear & Greed gauge?

It measures crowd emotion on a 0–100 scale, blended from volatility, momentum, social media, surveys, and search trends.

0–25 Extreme Fear — panic selling. Contrarians see opportunity: the crowd has given up, so downside is often exhausted.

25–45 Fear — caution dominates. Good entries form here if the trend is intact.

45–55 Neutral — no edge from sentiment. Follow price action.

55–75 Greed — optimism builds. Ride winners but tighten stops.

75–100 Extreme Greed — euphoria. The crowd is all-in, which means there's nobody left to buy. Time to trim.

NexIntel uses this as a contrarian input: extreme fear adds long bias, extreme greed adds short bias.
