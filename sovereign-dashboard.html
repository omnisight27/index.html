Predictions

A prediction is a hypothesis, stored before the outcome exists. The engine records what it expects, when it expects it, and how confident it is — then waits for reality to answer.

Outcomes

An outcome is the actual result, recorded later and linked to its prediction. One outcome per prediction — a second one is refused, because replacing the first would rewrite history.

Calibration Error

CE = Observed − Predicted. The error is computed by the engine, never typed in by hand. Loading calibration…

Drift

When a metric moves away from its baseline, the engine records it: baseline, current, change, status. Normal up to 5%, warning to 20%, critical past that. Foundational only — the advanced analytics come later, after real data exists.

Validation Status

Where validation stands today.

Experimental across the board — but no longer without evidence. The NexIntel track record is real validation data: a graded pick record with a measured win rate and calibration error (see Validation Center). What is still missing is the engine-side import of that record and the first validated metric.

What exists

Model Registry, Score Registry, Prediction Registry, Outcome Registry, Calibration Registry, Drift Registry — all operational, all audited, all in the engine. Plus a real graded track record in the live feeds.

What does not exist yet

The engine-side import of the track record (Phase A/B), real user ledgers in the engine, and the first validated metric. The engine has never measured a real ledger — and it will not pretend otherwise.

What turns it into science

Users, actions, outcomes, measurements, validation. The first real Opportunity that leads to a measurable improvement in a real user's ledger will be worth more than every document, sprint, and specification that came before it.

Roadmap
