Validation Center

See the uncertainty.

Most apps hide uncertainty. Freedom GP$ exposes it. Every model lives in a registry with a validation level — and right now, honestly, every one of them is Experimental. That is a real fact from the registry, not a demo figure.

Validation levels: Conceptual → Prototype → Experimental → Validated → Institutional. No score has reached Validated. The registry is the single source of truth for every score's formula, version, and status — which finally settles every future disagreement between specifications.

Predictions

A prediction is a hypothesis, stored before the outcome exists. The engine records what it expects, when it expects it, and how confident it is — then waits for reality to answer.

Outcomes

An outcome is the actual result, recorded later and linked to its prediction. One outcome per prediction — a second one is refused, because replacing the first would rewrite history.

Calibration Error

CE = Observed − Predicted. The error is computed by the engine, never typed in by hand. Illustrative example: predicted 100, observed 96, CE = −4. That is the machinery that turns guesses into measurements.

Drift

When a metric moves away from its baseline, the engine records it: baseline, current, change, status. Normal up to 5%, warning to 20%, critical past that. Foundational only — the advanced analytics come later, after real data exists.

Validation Status

Where validation stands today.

Experimental across the board. That is not a failure — it is the honest starting line. “Experimental” means the formula and bands are defined and the engine computes them on illustrative inputs. No real-world validation data exists yet. The machinery to collect it is built. What comes next is evidence.

What exists

Model Registry, Score Registry, Prediction Registry, Outcome Registry, Calibration Registry, Drift Registry — all operational, all audited, all in the engine.

What does not exist yet

Real users. Real predictions. Real outcomes. Real calibration. The engine has never measured a real ledger — and it will not pretend otherwise.

What turns it into science

Users, actions, outcomes, measurements, validation. The first real Opportunity that leads to a measurable improvement in a real user's ledger will be worth more than every document, sprint, and specification that came before it.
