How NexIntel Predictions Work

1. Ingest. Every 6 hours the engine pulls live data: 1,000 cryptocurrencies, 44 stocks & ETFs, gold/silver, forex, Fear & Greed sentiment, federal contract awards, and macro data (CPI, jobs, rates).

2. Features. Each asset gets 22 model signals — trend (MACD, moving averages), momentum (RSI, rate-of-change), volatility (Bollinger, ATR), mean-reversion (z-score), and risk (VaR proxy).

3. Ensemble score. Three layers combine: a regression model (optimal weights solved via least-squares), geometric pattern matching (how similar is today to past winners vs losers), and a regime tilt (risk-on boosts longs, risk-off damps them).

4. Psychology layer. Extreme greed → contrarian bearish bias. Extreme fear → contrarian bullish bias. Gold over $4,000 or a surging dollar trims confidence.

5. Thesis. Every pick gets entry/exit dates, stop-loss, take-profit, risk/reward, and expected return — plus position sizing via the Kelly criterion (starts at 0% until the model has enough scored history).

6. Remember. After each run, prior picks are scored against actual prices. Wins and losses train the model — weights optimize automatically once 22+ scored samples exist.

No model is right every time. Confidence % is the model's own uncertainty — higher is better, but nothing here is financial advice.
