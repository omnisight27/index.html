🎲 Kalshi Probability Watch

Kalshi is a regulated exchange where every price is the crowd's probability of a real event — a Fed decision, an inflation print, Bitcoin above a level by a date. We watch those prices, keep our own estimate next to them on crypto thresholds, and paper-trade the disagreements: when our number and the crowd's differ by 10+ points, a $300 paper position goes in the book and gets graded at settlement, same as every other call this system makes.
