Example
Event: Hilton Amex Paid Off

Yesterday

Hilton Amex remained one of the highest-interest liabilities in the financial route.

Today

Hilton Amex was paid in full.

Approximately $12,933.67 in debt was eliminated.

Funds were transferred from Ally through checking to complete the payoff.

This was a liability elimination event, not a spending event.

Tomorrow

The route is recalculated.

The next priority account becomes active.

Future interest costs continue to decline.
